Northwestern Formula Racing 2027:
Corner Brokers

For Northwestern Formula Racing's 2027 car, I'm designing the Corner Brokers: four small boards, one at each wheel, that read sensors on the suspension and brakes and send that data to the rest of the car. I'm the lead designer.

Altium Designer Schematic Design PCB Layout STM32 Strain Gauges CAN Bus Python

1. Overview

The Project

Northwestern Formula Racing's Data Acquisition (DAQ) subteam builds the electronics that collect data from the car. The Corner Brokers are part of that. Each wheel gets its own board, which reads the sensors at that corner and sends the readings over CAN, the network the car's electronics use to talk to each other.

The goal is to see how the suspension actually behaves under real driving loads. The team can use that data to check future designs, and it also gives the car the per-wheel load information it needs for torque vectoring (controlling how much power goes to each wheel).

2. Hardware

The Board

I'm designing the board in Altium Designer. It has to be small enough to mount at the wheel, about 38.5 × 21 mm, so a lot of the work is fitting everything into that space.

3. My Role

What I'm Working On

  • Designing the schematic and layout, including the circuits that clean up each sensor signal before it's measured
  • Improving on last year's board, which had strain readings that drifted over time and power parts that ran hot
  • Going through last year's driving data in Python to decide what the new board needed
  • Next up: ordering boards, assembling and testing them, writing firmware, and getting them on the car
